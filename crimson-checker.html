Site data

How accurate is this?

  • Fit stats are calculated from CCP's official static data () with every skill at level V, no implants, boosters, fleet bursts or overheating. The same rules the game uses are applied generically, so any ship, module, rig, charge, drone or subsystem can be pasted.
  • Not modelled: Reactive Armor Hardener adaptation (kept at its even base resists), Tech 3 destroyer modes, spool-up on Triglavian weapons (base damage shown), fighters, weapon tracking / missile application and range. DPS is the paper figure.
  • NPC stats (damage, damage types, neuts, HP, resists) are the real values for these NPC ships from the same static data. Which ships and how many spawn per room comes from the EVE University wiki's 2023 write-up, the closest documented layout. How many of them shoot you at the same time is the main uncertainty, so it is a slider: 60% is my default guess, 100% is the worst case. Drifter / Rogue Drone / Triglavian rift escalations are not included.
