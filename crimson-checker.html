How accurate is this?

  • Fit stats are calculated from CCP's official static data () with every skill at level V, no implants, boosters, fleet bursts or overheating. The same rules the game uses are applied generically, so any ship, module, rig, charge, drone or subsystem can be pasted.
  • Not modelled: Reactive Armor Hardener adaptation (kept at its even base resists), Tech 3 destroyer modes, spool-up on Triglavian weapons (base damage shown), fighters, weapon tracking / missile application and range. DPS is the paper figure.
  • NPC stats (damage, damage types, neuts, HP, resists) are the real values for these NPC ships from the same static data. Which ships and how many spawn per room comes from the EVE University wiki's 2023 write-up, the closest documented layout. How many of them shoot you at the same time is the main uncertainty, so it is a slider: 60% is my default guess, 100% is the worst case. Drifter / Rogue Drone / Triglavian rift escalations are not included.
  • PvP check needs an internet connection: it asks zKillboard for the recent losses of your hull, finds the hulls that did most of the killing, and gives each one the fit it most typically flies (taken from that hull's own recent losses). Your fit then duels each of them with the same engine. It is paper numbers only: no range, speed, tracking, kiting, overheating, drugs, implants or fleet boosts, and it assumes both ships simply shoot until one dies. Losses only show fits that died, so "typical" means what people fly, not what wins. Most real kills are gangs, which is why the gang size and how long you last against one are shown separately.
  • Wormhole sites (C3–C6) use the Sleepers' real stats from ESI and the wave layouts on the EVE University wiki. Each wave is checked on its own; the hardest one is picked by default and the slider starts at 100%, because a Sleeper wave all shoots you. Spawns the wiki lists as "possible" are counted. Sentry towers are counted only in the first wave, so add margin if you leave them up. Capital escalation waves are not included. Remote repairs between Sleepers slow your clear time and are included in it.
